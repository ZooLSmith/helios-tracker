ELIOS TRACKER

A live map of your Borderlands 2 game, in a browser tab. The level you're in, drawn from the game's own map, with your squad, the enemies, the loot and your missions moving on it as you play.

Keep it on a second screen, a phone or an OBS scene, or share it with your friends. It reads the game and draws nothing in it.

Install it Source on GitHub

What's on the map

Every kind of marker is a layer you can turn on or off, resize, label, or limit by distance and floor.

  • PlayersYou in yellow (or whoever you follow), the others in white with their names.
  • EnemiesRed diamonds, like the minimap's, with a health bar once they're hurt.
  • NPCs and alliesGreen rings. Vendors, stations and vault symbols have layers of their own.
  • Quest giversA mission to pick up, or one ready to hand in.
  • ObjectivesYour missions' waypoints, from the game: areas as dashed circles, spots as diamonds.
  • GearWeapons, shields, mods and relics on the ground, in their rarity's colour.
  • PickupsCash, ammo, health, eridium and mission items.
  • Chests and containersRed chests biggest. Looted ones move to a layer of their own; click one to see what it can drop.

Hover a marker for its name, health, distance and height; click it for the details.

More than a map

  • Player inspector. Click a player to see their gear, backpack and skill trees. Item cards use the game's own names, stats, parts and icons.
  • Mission log. Every mission of your playthrough: the story in order with the side missions each one unlocks, or grouped by area, with the rewards at your level.
  • Best now. Ranks the missions you can do right now by experience, cash or both, and says where to go next.
  • Skills. Each player's action skill (ready, running or cooling down) and their timed skill effects.
  • Areas and fog of war. The game's own area names and fog, over the places you haven't found yet.
  • Co-op. Only one player needs it. It works for the host and for clients, best on the host: why.
  • Your language. The page is in English or French. Names of items, skills and places come from the game, in the game's language.

Where to open it

  • A second screen, in any browser on the gaming PC.
  • A phone or tablet on your home network: turn on LAN access.
  • OBS, as a browser source with a see-through background: set it up.
  • The Steam overlay's browser, over the game itself: see how.

Built from your own game

The download holds no game files: no map images, no fonts, no icons, no text. The mod reads them from your own copy of Borderlands 2, live, while you play:

  • Maps come out of each level's package as you enter it.
  • Fonts are the game's own UI fonts, rebuilt from its files, so the page reads like the game's menus.
  • Icons (skills, manufacturers, elements, item types) are decoded from the game's art when the page first asks for them.
  • Names of items, skills, missions and places come from the running game, in its language.

What it pulls out stays on your PC, cached so the next session is instant. It's also why the DLCs you own show up without anything extra to install.

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't change your game or your save. It only reads.
  • It draws nothing in game, so nothing covers the HUD.
  • It doesn't go online. Your PC serves the page, and the page loads nothing from the internet.
